⚠ One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude — in Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia to the balcony at Jason's house and accept her meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it anyway: two minutes, zero risk. (PowerPyx, vi.ki)

The 100% checklist, in numbers

After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The checklist is a selection, not everything — these are the numbers it asks for. (PowerPyx, KeenGamer)

Roadmap figures: PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours in one playthrough, with no online trophies and no mission replay. Push Square puts the story at 60–80 hours before the cleanup begins.
